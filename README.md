Project Description:

Langfly is a platform where users learn new languages through lessons, flashcards, daily challenges, and live practice sessions.

Design Idea: Fresh, clean food aesthetic, weekly menu selector, recipe preview cards, dietary filter tags, subscription management dashboard.

Design Source File:

Acquiring a new language often presents significant hurdles for self-directed learners due to low long-term engagement, fragmented study tools, and a lack of real-time conversational practice. Traditional learning methods and conventional digital platforms frequently isolate theoretical knowledge- such as vocabulary drills and grammar lessons-from practical application, leading to rapid drop-off rates and minimal speaking confidence. Learners struggle to maintain consistent daily habits without clear visual progression, interactive gamification, and seamless access to interactive practice environments. Langfly brings structured learning roadmaps, interactive flashcards, gamified daily challenges, and real-time live practice booking together in one platform to help you retain consistently and build fluent conversations.
